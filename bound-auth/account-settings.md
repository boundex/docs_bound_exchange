# Account Settings

Access your account settings from **My Account** in the navigation.

From here you can manage:

* **Bound wallet authentication** - passkey and security settings
* **Linked wallets** - view Bound Auth wallets and imported wallets
* **Security settings** - update passkey, add backup auth methods
* **Preferences** - notifications and display settings
