## Overview

Standard DeFi-style collateralized loans with Bound's UX - deposit BTC as collateral, borrow stablecoins up to a defined LTV ratio.

Unlike liquidation-free loans, overcollateralized loans include standard liquidation mechanics if your collateral ratio falls below the threshold.
