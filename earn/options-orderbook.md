## Overview

The Bound options orderbook is a retail-facing interface for buying calls and puts on BTC - the first self-custodial BTC options orderbook.

## Key features

* **Lower minimums** than Deribit or CME - accessible to retail traders
* **Self-custodial** - you maintain custody throughout
* **Defined-risk** - every position has a known maximum loss

## Who it's for

* Retail options traders
* Degens looking for leveraged directional exposure
* TradFi crossover users priced out of institutional platforms
* Anyone wanting to hedge or speculate on BTC price with a defined risk profile
