## 2. Nature of the Platform

Bound Exchange is a full-stack, self-custodial DeFi platform purpose-built for Bitcoin and applicable to other networks. The platform provides three core services built on a non-custodial foundation:

### 2.1 Automated Market Making (AMM) - Swaps

Bound Exchange operates an Automated Market Maker protocol for Bitcoin and Bitcoin tokens (e.g., Runes) that executes trades on Bitcoin mainnet and leverages smart contracts on the Sonic Network to calculate and propagate trade quotes and liquidity. This enables two primary activities:

* **Automated Market Making:** Users may earn yield on their BTC and Runes by depositing assets into Bound Exchange Agency Pools within a customizable price range. These users are known as Liquidity Providers ("LPs").
* **Instant Swaps:** Users may buy or sell Runes with near-instant execution, with settlement occurring at the end of each Bitcoin block. These users are known as Traders.

### 2.2 Peer-to-Peer RFQ Lending

Bound Exchange operates a non-custodial, peer-to-peer lending protocol through which lenders may provide stablecoin liquidity and borrowers may obtain Bitcoin-collateralized loans. Key characteristics include:

* **Request for Quote (RFQ) Mechanics:** Borrowers submit loan requests specifying principal, term, collateral, and proposed APR. Lenders, operating through automated Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) infrastructure, respond to RFQs based on pre-configured risk policies.
* **Atomic Settlement:** Loan origination is settled via an atomic Partially Signed Bitcoin Transaction (PSBT) co-signed by all parties. All transfers settle together or not at all.
* **No-Liquidation Term Loans:** Loans are fixed-rate and fixed-term. Bound Exchange cannot force-sell or liquidate collateral during the loan term. Upon maturity, borrowers may repay principal and interest to reclaim their collateral, or elect to walk away, in which case the lender claims the BTC collateral pursuant to the escrow terms.
* **BTC Escrow:** Collateral is held in a 2-of-2 multisig escrow with a timelock. The borrower is always a required co-signer during the loan term and grace period. After expiry of the grace period, the lender may claim collateral unilaterally if repayment has not been made.
* **Lender Automation:** Lenders may configure a TEE-hosted agent to respond to RFQs autonomously based on a pre-set risk policy. Idle lender funds earn the short-duration treasury rate. When matched, lenders earn the agreed lender APR.

### 2.3 Investing and Capital Deployment

Bound Exchange provides an integrated investing interface through which users may deploy capital into major asset classes directly from their self-custodial account. Swaps and cross-chain transactions executed through this interface are subject to the fee and risk disclosures set out in these Terms.

This wallet is a 2-of-2 multisig between the user and Bound Exchange, in which Bound Exchange's co-signing authority has a predetermined expiration date, ensuring users can always reclaim custody of their assets.

## 3. Agency Pool Mechanics

Users may transfer BTC and/or Bitcoin tokens from their trading wallet to Bound Exchange Agency Pools. Transfers include instructions specifying a desired fee tier and price range.

* Pricing is determined using the constant product formula (x x y = k) within the specified range.
* Liquidity Providers earn a proportional share of trading fees generated by the pool. Fee tiers are set by the Liquidity Provider and may vary by pool. Fees accrue automatically and are claimable at any time.
* Bound Exchange charges a platform fee on fee earnings, as specified in official Bound Exchange documentation. Bound Exchange reserves the right to alter the percentage of fee earnings taken as a platform fee.
* Users may remove liquidity at any time by signing a transaction on the Bitcoin blockchain. Upon withdrawal, users receive their proportional share of pool assets plus accrued fees, subject to the pool's state at the time of withdrawal.
